Our Specialist Categories

Stamps

From Commonwealth classics to rare Asian issues, our philatelic specialists bring the full depth of Stanley Gibbons expertise to the Singapore market — buying, selling and appraising collections of all sizes.

Coins

Our coins team covers ancient, world and Asian coinage with a focus on the APAC region. Whether you are buying, selling or seeking a valuation, our specialists are on hand to assist.

Banknotes

Led by Jeremy Lee, our banknote specialist brings deep knowledge of Asian and world paper money. From rare colonial issues to modern rarities, we offer buying, selling and expert valuation services.

Expert Valuations

What Is Your Collection Worth?

Our Singapore specialists offer professional valuations for stamps, coins, banknotes and philatelic material of all kinds. Whether you hold a single rarity or an entire estate collection, we provide accurate, honest assessments backed by 170 years of expertise.
